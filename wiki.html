Wiki

The Wiki is the assistant's knowledge base. It keeps skills, tool descriptions, papers, manuals and your own history in searchable libraries, so the assistant can look things up and cite where they came from. It is built on WeKnora, and runs on your computer.

The Wiki is reference, not authority. Its text is never an instruction, live tool availability or approval, and no Wiki process runs a microscope tool. The Harness stays in charge of tools and execution.

Libraries

Library Holds Who can read
Shared - Harness Reference Skills, public MCP tool schemas, Harness guidance Everyone
Shared - Research Library Papers, equipment manuals, scientific methods Everyone
Personal - Custom Capabilities Your own skills and MCP metadata You
Personal - Documents Your private uploads You
Personal - Runs Workflow and event evidence, including failures You
Personal - Conversations Your chat history You

Personal knowledge is never promoted to a shared library automatically. Sharing is an administrator's decision, and what gets shared is a reviewed, redacted lesson, not raw history. The full rules are in knowledge boundaries.

What goes in

  • Skills and tools. On startup and whenever they change, skills and mcp__* tool schemas are mirrored as documents. These are generated copies: edit the original, not the copy.
  • Conversations and runs. Selected events are copied into your personal libraries. Passwords, tokens, keys and images are removed first. Each document is labelled as reference data, and user and assistant claims are marked as unverified.
  • Documents. Upload them in the Wiki, or set WEKNORA_IMPORT_DIR to a folder of Markdown or text files, which is checked every 30 seconds.

How the assistant uses it

Tool Use
weknora_knowledge_lookup Search one scope: capabilities, research, documents, runs or conversations
weknora_wiki_search, weknora_wiki_read_page Search and read Wiki pages
weknora_sync_status Read the state of history sync

A lookup tries Wiki pages first and falls back to the original documents. Pages are withheld if they have no source, are drafts, or cite a source that is missing, processing, or newer than the page. This checks freshness, not scientific truth, so the assistant should still confirm against the live skill or tool before acting.

In the app

The app carries WeKnora Lite and gives it a private folder in the app data, with a local SQLite database, listening on 127.0.0.1:8080. A Wiki already installed on the computer is used instead. Embeddings use the HU model llm5 and page writing uses llm3, so building the Wiki needs those servers to be reachable.

Open it from the control panel. Chat stays in the Harness: WeKnora does not hold any conversation with the model.

What it does not do yet

  • History is stored, not distilled. Nothing yet turns conversations into short, lasting memories.
  • Libraries are organised by person, not by instrument.

Both are listed under problems to be solved.
